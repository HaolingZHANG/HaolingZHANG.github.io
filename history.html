How did this initiative emerge and evolve?

We outline the key milestones of this initiative to illustrate how it matured over time.

  • Triggering event 2024-05-15
    Science reported on the tension surrounding the publication of AlphaFold3. Google DeepMind and Nature have come under fire for offering only restricted access to the program and failing to release the computational code underlying it. In November of the same year, Google DeepMind released an accessible version of AlphaFold3, which mitigated this tension.
  • Preliminary attempt 2024-11-08
    A mini-review from AI4BioMedicine Lab examining established research software principles was published, followed by discussions on two supplementary principles to further strengthen research software practices.
  • Initial stakeholder engagement 2024-12-06
    Building on the supplementary principles articulated in the mini-review, a new research software initiative began to take shape. In the following months, a growing number of stakeholders expressed interest in engaging in dialogue.
  • First coordinated discussion 2025-03-04
    The first foundational document was developed to facilitate discussions among stakeholders across different time zones. This round of discussions focused on the background of the initiative, as well as the rationale for the conceptual model "Evidence-Oriented Programming" and the operational strategy "Evidence Chain Formalization".
  • Second coordinated discussion 2025-07-08
    The objectives of the first coordinated discussion were achieved. Subsequent discussions on "Evidence Chain Formalization" focused on its applicability across different academic fields, and how it may help mitigate trust gaps when external disclosure barriers arise.
  • Case study release 2025-09-29
    The first code repository aligned with "Evidence Chain Formalization" successfully passed peer review and was accepted in principle by Nature Communications for publication. This case provided a substantive test of the core structure of Evidence Chain Formalization and generated valuable insights into its implementation and adoption.
  • Infrastructure collaboration 2025-10-23
    In addressing education and evaluation supports, as well as potential increasing computational and storage demands, implementation-oriented partners were engaged, including NVIDIA and Digital Science.
  • Adopter interviews 2025-12-04
    Following the convergence of the second coordinated discussion, one-on-one interviews were initiated with prospective adopters most suited to assume the initiative's workload, with the aim of gathering focused feedback, particularly regarding factors influencing adoption decisions.
